AlphaForge AI is an AI-powered market intelligence platform designed for traders, investors, and analysts. It combines real-time market data, AI-driven research, portfolio analytics, news intelligence, and risk management into a single premium platform.

The goal is to provide institutional-quality insights through an intuitive and modern interface.
